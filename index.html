How it works

Three simple ways to be part of Bridge & Bite.

1

Eat

Find the truck, grab a burger or fries, and taste the neighbourhood side of the brand. Good food comes first — that’s how we earn the right to tell the rest of the story.

2

Book the truck

Hire us for events, markets, or workplace catering. You’ll get solid food service — and a clear, honest story about training and jobs.

3

Join as a trainee

Interested in kitchen and service skills while building a fresh start? Ask about training shifts. We talk dignity and real work — not slogans.
